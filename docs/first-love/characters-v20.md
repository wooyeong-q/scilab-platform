# Four selectable protagonists

The heroine remains Han Seoa. A player chooses Seo Yunjae, Kang Doha, Cha Siwoo,
or the new Jeong Minwoo before a new solo play or class join. This is the lead
on that player's screen; it is independent of the cooperative seat and does not
change puzzle answers, permissions, or shared progress. The same character may
be selected by several players. Existing saves default to Yunjae.

Character selection is saved with the local story presentation. Resuming a save
uses its own lead even if a different lead was subsequently selected on the home
screen. Teacher previews use the currently selected lead. The lead occupies story
slot 0; if Doha or Siwoo is selected, Yunjae fills that character's supporting
slot. Names in narration and story props are substituted simultaneously, without
rewriting teacher-authored puzzle text. The story and puzzles are shared routes,
not four separately authored campaigns.

## Design and image production

Built-in image generation was used for four portraits and four expression
atlases. Original PNGs were converted to WebP without changing dimensions or
alpha. Runtime files are `public/labs/first-love/assets/{id}-v20.webp` and
`{id}-expressions-v20.webp`, where id is yunjae, doha, siwoo, or minwoo.
The 1024 × 1536 expression atlases contain four 512 × 768 cells: neutral,
nervous, surprised/concerned, and smile. Existing story expression cues map to
these cells, so no redesigned male character falls back to an old face.

Prompt set: polished Korean romance webtoon illustration, youthful Korean
schoolboy, school uniform, head through upper thighs, clear margins, transparent
alpha background, no scenery, text, logos, or borders.

- Yunjae: reserved, neat blue-black side-parted hair, navy piped school blazer,
  white shirt, navy tie, charcoal trousers, closed beige notebook.
- Doha: friendly extrovert, warm wavy brown hair, broad cheerful smile, oatmeal
  cardigan over white shirt and navy tie, charcoal trousers, casual hello wave.
- Siwoo: athletic baseball-player atmosphere in school uniform, short textured
  black hair with tapered sides and naturally lifted fringe, no bowl/chestnut
  cut, rolled white shirt sleeves, loosened navy tie, charcoal trousers.
- Minwoo: attentive observer, thin round glasses, tidy ash-brown side-parted
  hair, charcoal sweater vest, white shirt, navy tie, compact navy notebook.

Each expression atlas was generated by editing its corresponding portrait:
preserve identity, hair, clothing and proportions; use an equal 2 × 2 grid;
keep framing consistent within cells; vary the four facial emotions only.
Doha's atlas uses relaxed arms to suit ordinary conversation.
